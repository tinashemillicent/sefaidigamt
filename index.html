About

Curious since form three, still saying “I do” to computer science.

HARARE · ZIMBABWE

I still remember discovering my love for CS & IT. In form three, my teacher let me stay behind in the lab working through Form 6 coding exercises — and that was the moment I said “I do” to everything computer science and IT.

Since then I've finished a Bachelor of Technology in Information Technology and a Diploma in Big Data Analytics, and picked up range across data analysis, visualisation, systems design, troubleshooting, programming and admin work — with plenty more I'm still grasping.

I write weekly to stay accountable to the goals I set, and I'm happiest connecting with like-minded people and adding real value to the teams I join.
